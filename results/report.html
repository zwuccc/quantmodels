How to read this

Walk forward: for each test year, settings are picked using only earlier years, then run on that year alone and stitched together. The holdout (last two years) was run once with settings fixed before it was loaded.

Survivorship bias cannot be removed with free data. The equal weight universe row shows how much any stock strategy gets just from owning today's survivors. Beating SPY by less than that gap is not evidence of skill.

Fundamentals are used from their SEC filing date, never their period end date. Missing data means the name was skipped and logged, never filled.
